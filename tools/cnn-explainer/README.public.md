# CNN Explainer (interactive overview)

[CNN Explainer](https://poloclub.github.io/cnn-explainer/) is an interactive visualization
of a small convolutional neural network by Zijie J. Wang, Robert Turko, Omar Shaikh,
Haekyu Park, Nilaksh Das, Fred Hohman, Minsuk Kahng and Duen Horng (Polo) Chau
(Polo Club of Data Science, Georgia Tech), released under the MIT License (see
[`LICENSE.txt`](LICENSE.txt)).

> Wang et al., "CNN Explainer: Learning Convolutional Neural Networks with Interactive
> Visualization", IEEE TVCG, 2020. https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.15004

These files are generated by `tools/cnn-explainer/build.sh`; do not edit them by hand.
They hold only the interactive overview, built from the upstream source and mounted
directly into the Knowledge page (`/[locale]/knowledge/cnn`) by
`src/components/knowledge/cnn-explainer.tsx`:

- `embed.js`: the overview and detail views (Svelte), exposing
  `window.CnnExplainer.mount(element)`.
- `embed.css`: its styles, Bulma 0.8.0 included, all scoped under `.cnn-explainer`.
- `assets/`: the trained TinyVGG model and the sample images.
- `vendor/`: d3 5.16.0, TensorFlow.js 1.0.0 and Font Awesome Free 5.3.1 from their npm
  packages; licences in `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt`.
